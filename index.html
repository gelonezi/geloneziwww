03 / How I work

Four principles I ship by

  • P-01

    Agents draft, I decide

    Claude Code, Copilot, Cursor and MCP-connected sub-agents, used inside guardrails: specs first, small diffs, and every change reviewed before merge. That's how a two-month postgrad phase took about 20 hours.

  • P-02

    Decisions in writing

    RFCs and ADRs turn hallway decisions into durable context. New engineers learn why, not just what, and architecture stops depending on who was in the room.

  • P-03

    Observable, or it isn't done

    Metrics, traces and alerts ship with the feature. That's what turned zero-incident cutovers into a measurement instead of a hope.

  • P-04

    Build teams that outgrow me

    Mentoring from intern to senior, onboarding that sticks, and knowledge transfer deep enough that a client's own team can take over the codebase.
